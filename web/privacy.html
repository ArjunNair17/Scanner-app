Privacy Policy

Protein Scanner: AI Calories photographs a plate and estimates calories and protein.

  • There is no login and no account.
  • Meal history stays on your iPhone (SQLite). We do not sync meals to a server.
  • On Scan, a resized JPEG is sent to our Worker, then to Google Gemini. We discard the photo after the response.
  • A random device_id is stored on-device and sent as x-device-id for rate limits only.
  • No HealthKit. No barcode database. No advertising identifier sale.
